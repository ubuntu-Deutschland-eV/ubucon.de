Friday 18 November: get together

Museum Tour

We've registered a guided tour (in English) through the Zeche Zollverein. It's a former coal-mine and a important historical object in the Ruhr-Area. See their website about the tour for more details. The tour is limited to 20 people. If you want to attend, please make sure to type in your name in this form. We'll close the form when 20 people registered.

Update 18th November 8:30: We've reached the limit of 20 attendees.

  • Meeting time: 15:30
  • Meeting point: RUHR.VISITORCENTER Essen, Areal A [Schacht XII], Kohlenwäsche [A14]
  • Address: Gelsenkirchener Straße 181, 45309 Essen
  • Tramstation: Essen Zollverein, get on Tram 107 (Direction: Gelsenkirchen Hbf) (journeyplanner)

Social Event

The first social event is on friday in the restaurant of the Unperfekthaus. The entrance fee for the Unperfekthaus which includes hot and soft drinks is 6,90€. The dinner as a buffet costs another 15€. This must be paid directly to the Unperfekthaus.

It starts at 19:00!
